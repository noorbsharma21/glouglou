Everything on the shelf is farmed without herbicide and made without additions past a little sulphur at bottling, and then only when the grower decided it needed it. Some of it is cloudy. Some of it smells like cider for ten minutes and then stops.

We taste every bottle before it goes out, and when we have got it wrong we will tell you so and open something else.

BEHIND THE COUNTER

Four of us.
Ask any of the four.

There are no seats and there is no list to study. There is a counter, four of us behind it, and whatever is open that day. Say roughly what you want — cold, red, sour, cheap, a present — and one of us will put a glass in your hand before you pay for anything.

Skins

RACK 02
WE SAY

WE SAY

Twenty-one days, properly chewy. Marmalade and wet hay. It will fight anything you put beside it except a hard cheese.

WE SAY

Fourteen days and a whisper of oxidation. Walnut and green apple. If you have never had a skin-contact savagnin, start on this one.
